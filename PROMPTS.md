# How AI Was Used

AI was mainly used as a development assistant rather than generating the complete application.

I first worked on understanding the project, making implementation decisions, writing and modifying the code, selecting the required content/assets, and testing the application myself. AI was mainly used for debugging, explaining unfamiliar code, reviewing specific implementations, suggesting small improvements, and helping with the final checks.

The final implementation was reviewed and adjusted manually based on the reference page and the behavior required by the assignment.
